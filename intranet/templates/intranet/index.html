Navigation

Use the top navigation bar to access the two main sections of the portal: Entries and Tariff.

Entries

The Entries page shows all quotes, bookings and final itineraries pending a reply from our team. Each row represents one version of a file.

  • Status: Quote, Booking or Final Itinerary.
  • Progress: indicates at what stage our team is working on your file (from "Not started" to "Finalized").
  • Contact us: click the envelope icon on any row to send a message to our team directly about that file. We will receive your message and reply by email.
  • Use the Columns button to show or hide columns, and the search box to filter by any text.
  • Each staff member is identified by a username code and a colour. Full names, emails and progress stage descriptions are available in the References box next to the title.
Tariff

The Tariff section shows rates for accommodation and services in Argentina for the selected season.

  • Switch between Accommodation and Services tabs and select a Destination. You can also change the season if more than one is available.

  • Accommodation
  • Use the search box to filter by property name.
  • Properties are grouped by area or neighbourhood. Click on any group name to expand its rates by period.
  • Click More info on any hotel to see photos and a full description.
  • A next to a property name means it is recommended by Aliwen.
  • A next to a category name indicates an important note — click it to read it.

  • Services
  • Use the search box to filter by tour, transfer or experience name.
  • Services are grouped by category. Click on any category name to expand its rates by period.
  • Click on the name of any tour, transfer or experience to see a full description and more details.
  • Each service has a timing icon indicating when it runs: Morning, Afternoon, Evening.

  • Additional features
  • Use the PDF button to generate a printable rate sheet for accommodation or services.
  • Use the Excel button to download a spreadsheet of the rates currently displayed.
  • Use the Calendar button to visit the Special Dates section and download calendars by year.
  • Use the History button to view the History of Changes and see the latest rate updates.
  • Use the button to open the Hotel Rate Comparison tool and compare rooms across different properties.
