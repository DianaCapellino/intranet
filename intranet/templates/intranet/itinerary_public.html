Validity & rates
  • This document is a quotation — bookings have not been made. Hotels, restaurants and other services are subject to availability upon request.
  • Valid for 15 days from the date sent, and only for the dates and passenger count described.
  • Rates are subject to change with the exchange rate.
Inclusions
  • Lodging includes breakfast unless another meal plan is stated.
  • National Park, museum and reserve entrance fees are included only when explicitly detailed.
  • Private transfers/excursions use exclusive vehicles; shared services are with other travellers. Standard duration applies — extra hours are billed separately.
Good to know before you travel
  • Check hotel check-in/check-out times against flight schedules — early check-in / late check-out is not included unless stated.
  • Some excursions are weather-dependent.
  • Tips for luggage handling and meals are not included.
  • International and domestic flights and medical assistance insurance are quoted separately.
Payment & cancellation policy
  • Full pre-payment is required before arrival; hotels requiring prepayment will be requested separately by due date.
  • Cancellation policy and penalties depend on how close to arrival the cancellation is made, plus any supplier cancellation penalties.
